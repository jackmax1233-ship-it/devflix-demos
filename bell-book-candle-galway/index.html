Community

More than a shop

Bell Book and Candle is woven into Galway's cultural calendar — a gathering place as much as a bookshop.

October 2026

Galway Cartoon Festival workshop

The shop welcomed the Galway Cartoon Festival for a cartoon workshop — artists, sketchbooks and a crowd spilling out by the Small Crane. This is what the West End does best.

Record Store Day

Doors open at 8am

For Record Store Day the shutters go up at 8am sharp — exclusive stock, signed copies and pressings you won't find anywhere else in the West. The queue starts early; the crates don't last.
