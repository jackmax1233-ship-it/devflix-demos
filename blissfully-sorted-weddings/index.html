Coordination, not decoration

Blissfully Sorted Weddings coordinates the day; it is a planning and coordination service, not a décor or floristry service. Three services are listed:

Bridal dressing

Help on the wedding morning, so getting ready is about getting ready — not worrying about getting ready.

Day coordination

Someone bringing everything together on the day itself: supplier timings, details and behind-the-scenes moments, handled for you.

Final-weeks coordination

Support in the final weeks before the wedding, tying the plans together before the day arrives.
