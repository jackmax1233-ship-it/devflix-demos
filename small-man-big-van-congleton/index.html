What we do

Big enough for a house. Small enough for a single item.

Every service below is work this van has actually done — shown in the photos from recent jobs, not a generic list.

House moves

Sale-and-purchase moves, including moves between two Congleton properties and longer moves — with larger crews and a second van when the job calls for it.

Small & office moves

Office equipment out of business premises, and smaller moves for returning customers — moved to temporary storage or straight to the new place.

Single items & collections

One-off collections and deliveries: furniture, a shed from a local store, auction items — collected and delivered around Congleton and beyond.

Storage runs

Moves in and out of storage in Congleton, including runs from Apex Self Storage — and deliveries onward from storage to a new home.

Event logistics

Two-day event jobs: loaded in Congleton, set up at the venue, and brought back again — a different kind of job, handled the same careful way.

Multi-day pack & move

Full-house pack-and-move jobs over two or three days — packed, loaded, moved and set up, beds built at the other end.

Based in Congleton

Local, insured, and used to the tricky days.

Completion days rarely run to plan. Recent jobs have waited on a 3pm key release, worked around another firm emptying the purchase property, and still fitted in an extra collection before the day was out.

That is the kind of day this service is built for: organised loading, patience when completions slip, and the job finished properly.
