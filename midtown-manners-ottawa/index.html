About Adam

A trainer who trains in the real world

"Helping Humans and Dogs Build Better Relationships."

Adam is Midtown Manners — an independent, owner-operated dog trainer in Ottawa, Ontario. His training happens on sidewalks, porches, parks and markets: loose-leash walking through the neighbourhood, calm settle practice on the porch, socialization out at places like Parkdale Market.

His clients are real neighbourhood dogs — from reactivity cases like Styx to brand-new puppies like Penny — and every session is built around the dog in front of him.

Worth knowing: Adam doesn't publish a price list — every plan is quoted personally, so DM him with your dog's details for pricing.
