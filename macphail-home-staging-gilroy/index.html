Meet Jessica

Jessica MacPhail is the founder of MacPhail Home Staging & Design — and a Gilroy native, born and raised in the town she now stages homes in.

For her, a home is never just about how it looks. It is about how a space feels, the memories made inside it, and bringing out what each room does best. That belief is what turned a lifelong love of intentional, beautiful spaces into this business.

She works with local realtors looking to lift a listing, homeowners preparing for a move, and anyone who simply loves thoughtful interiors.
