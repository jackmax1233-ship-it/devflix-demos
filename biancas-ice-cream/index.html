Our Story

Swiss craft, Doha heart

Bianca's is a Swiss ice cream maker with its home in Doha. We churn every batch the artisanal way — slowly, carefully, and with ingredients we'd happily serve our own family.

From classic creamy scoops to dairy-free options and our probiotic frozen kefir, everything is made to be enjoyed the Swiss way: unhurried, and preferably with good company.
