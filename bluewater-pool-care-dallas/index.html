Services

Weekly pool cleaning

Recurring cleaning, water balancing, equipment checks and service reports on a fixed weekly route.

Filter cleaning

Cartridge filter cleaning — most pools need it every few months. If your pressure gauge is running high, it’s time. Cartridges are removed, every pleat hosed down, inspected for wear or tears, and reassembled.

Rule of thumb from Bluewater: clean when pressure reads about 8–10 PSI above its clean starting point.

Green pool cleanup & one-time cleaning

A reset for pools behind on service, hit by a storm, or left unattended — then a regular schedule to keep the result.
