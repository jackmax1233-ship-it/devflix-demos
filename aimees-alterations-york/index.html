Services

Bridal alterations, done properly

One specialist, one gown at a time. Aimée works on the structure of the dress — not just the hem — so it fits, supports and moves with you.

01

Bridal fittings

Your gown pinned to your shape while you wear it — every adjustment planned around how you stand, sit and dance in the dress.

02

Pinning & reshaping

Necklines, skirts and silhouettes reshaped to flatter your figure — from subtle tweaks to full transformations.

03

Structural bodice work

Boning, cups and internal structure adjusted so the bodice holds you comfortably from morning to midnight.

04

Bustles & transformations

Bustles added for the evening, and before-and-after gown transformations — see the work below.
