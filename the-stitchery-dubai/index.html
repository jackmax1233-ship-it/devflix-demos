About The Stitchery

More than tailoring. It’s a better you.

“Your style, crafted in the comfort of your home.”

The Stitchery is the UAE’s premier private home tailoring service. Instead of asking you to come to the shop, their tailors bring the fitting room to you — precise measurements, professional craftsmanship, and personalised attention to every detail.

From elegant custom outfits to perfectly altered designer dresses, every alteration, every stitch and every detail is shaped around you — because the right fit doesn’t just change an outfit. It changes how you carry yourself.
