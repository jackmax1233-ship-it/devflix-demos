Meet Karina

Care that starts with listening.

Karina Meidell is a Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) based in Toronto’s west end, building a practice grounded in trust and genuine care.

Her passion for massage was ignited by her own journey with chronic pain — giving her a personal understanding of how much the right treatment can help.

A quality treatment is built around an inclusive and safe space where you feel heard and supported.

Every session is a personalized, evidence-informed approach to massage, shaped by your goals — to support injury recovery, pain management, and overall well-being.
