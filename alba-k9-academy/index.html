The training philosophy

Patience over pressure

"This is your dog's first time on this planet."

Some "behavioural" issues are really just a misunderstanding between you and your dog. Don't be afraid to scale back during sessions or take a totally different approach entirely.

Work through life's challenges as a team — and remember that something as simple as crossing the road can be a massive challenge for your dog. Take them through it like it's their first time here. Because it is.

The reason behind the logo

BART 8318

Look closely at the dog's harness in the Alba K9 badge and you'll spot an Easter egg: BART 8318.

Bart was a British Army protection dog who served throughout Operation Herrick in Afghanistan — trained in crowd control and wind scenting, a highly capable working dog.

But he was so much more than that. Bart took a skinny 21-year-old handler — Tam, on his first operational tour — under his wing. He showed him the mistakes he was making and the things he was doing well, working through training problems together in some pretty isolated parts of Helmand Province. On bad days, Bart switched into full affection mode, making his way around the teams they were attached to — a bit of comfort, a reminder of home.

As he got older, Bart returned to the Defence Animal Centre in the UK, where he helped train the next generation of handlers — just as he'd done for Tam.

This venture wouldn't exist without him. Bart taught lessons that went far beyond working-dog training: trust, teamwork, resilience, and the importance of the bond between a dog and their handler. Those lessons stayed with Tam — and they're what Alba K9 Academy is built on.
