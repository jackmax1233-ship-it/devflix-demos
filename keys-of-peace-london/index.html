About

A pianist and certified teacher in London

Keys of Peace is led by an ABRSM Certified Piano Teacher and pianist based in London. Her posts reach a warm, engaged community — recent performances have drawn hundreds of likes and dozens of comments from piano lovers.

Lessons are private and personalised, welcoming complete beginners as well as players looking to improve their skills — it is never too late to learn piano.
