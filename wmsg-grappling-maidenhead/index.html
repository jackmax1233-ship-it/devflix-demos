What We Train

Three disciplines, one room — grappling for competition and for fitness, taught as a single programme.

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Ground grappling built on leverage, position and submissions — the core of the club's programme, coached to black-belt standard.

Submission Grappling

No-gi grappling with the full submission game: takedowns, control and finishes, trained live with regular partners.

Freestyle Wrestling

Takedowns, movement and mat control — the standing game that rounds out the grappling programme.

Head Coach

Keith Ellis — BJJ black belt and ADCC European trials master champion, per the club's own bio. Coaching is hands-on: small-room sessions where regular training partners matter, which is why the club is actively looking for more of them.

This site is about the club, not one person — but if you're wondering who'll be coaching your first session, that's Keith.

New? You're Welcome Here

The club's sessions are described as a mix "from complete beginners" — you don't need experience, fitness or a gi to come and see a session first.

  • Complete beginners welcome — sessions include people at every level.
  • Regular training partners wanted — if you stick around, you'll be part of the room, not a spectator.
  • No published membership price — ask the club directly (see below).
