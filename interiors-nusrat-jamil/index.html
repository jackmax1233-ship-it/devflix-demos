The Studio

Two decades of quiet, confident design

Founded in 2005 and led by interior designer Nusrat Jamil, the studio shapes homes across Lahore — from 1-Kanal family homes to 2-Kanal modern residences.

Every interior is composed around the people who live in it: bespoke layouts, custom curtains, signature wall art and furniture designed as one complete, timeless whole.

— Signature Interiors by NJ
