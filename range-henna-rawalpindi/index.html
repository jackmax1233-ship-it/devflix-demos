About Bismah

A wedding-season favourite in the twin cities

Rang-e-Henna is the bridal mehndi practice of Bismah, working across Rawalpindi and Islamabad. Her feed is a diary of wedding seasons — one bride after another, each design drawn to suit the bride wearing it, from classic full-hand bridal work to Mughal-inspired art.

Clients give her creative freedom and, as she puts it, end up becoming family by the end of the wedding. Bookings and inquiries are handled by DM on Instagram.

Follow @rangehennabybismah for her latest brides.
