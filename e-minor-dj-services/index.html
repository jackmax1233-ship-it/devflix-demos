The DJ behind the decks

One DJ. Every detail.

“I am an experienced and professional DJ for all your event needs.”

E Minor DJ Services is run by Emmett Minor, based on the outskirts of the beautiful Ottawa region in Carleton Place, Ontario. When you book E Minor, Emmett himself shows up, sets up, reads the room and keeps your night moving.

Couples on WeddingWire name Emmett personally and mention both his wedding DJ and photo booth services — and the directory holds a 5.0 rating across 18 named reviews.

Recent venues include the locks in Perth, Ecotay, Orchard View, the Oakfield rugby park, Almonte’s Old Town Hall, Timber Run Golf and the Maberly Riverhouse.
