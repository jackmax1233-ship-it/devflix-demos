What we do

Repair, restore, reimagine

One workshop, three crafts: keeping beloved shoes alive, and building new silhouettes that never existed before.

Repair

Shoe repair & restoration

Resoling, shining and full restoration — "we've been in the business of extending the lives of shoes for decades," as Boris puts it. Beloved pairs get a second life, not a landfill.

Custom

Custom sneaker design

One-off sneaker designs and wild EVSR platforms built to order — from miniature platforms to club-kid stage pieces that have walked runways and red carpets.

Modification

Footwear modification

Rebuilt silhouettes — thigh-high and knee-high platforms on Converse, zippers, fringes, fur. The workshop's museum of reinvented samples keeps growing.

The workshop

From the East Village days

"Reinventing shoes" — that's the job description at 1073 Broadway.

East Village Shoe Repair is the artisan workshop of designer Boris Zuborev, with collaborator Eugene Finkelberg — a bench where classic cobbling meets experimental sneaker design.

It started in Manhattan's East Village; today the Bushwick workshop builds custom footwear, restores designer pairs, and ships the Zubo adjustable platform to customers who find the shop through Instagram, word of mouth — and runways.
