About

Henna art from Doha, shared design by design

KH Henna Artist is a Doha-based henna artist specialising in Arabic designs — the flowing florals, vines and fine linework her Instagram feed is full of. New designs are posted there regularly, from deep-staining bridal pieces to light, simple patterns.

She takes bookings for weddings, parties and events, and offers home service, so you can have your henna done where the celebration is happening.

Follow @kh_henna_artist on Instagram to see the latest work.
