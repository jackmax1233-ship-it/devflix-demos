Dancing in Glasgow since 1999.

For over two decades, Dancebeat Studio has been a place to learn, train and perform. Whether dance is a hobby or a competitive pursuit, the studio offers training that fits the dancer — recreational classes and competitive training, side by side.

Classes

Two ways to dance at Dancebeat — for fun, fitness and friendship, or for the stage and competition floor.

Recreational training

Dance for enjoyment and confidence, in a friendly studio atmosphere. A great way to learn to dance, keep active and be part of the Dancebeat community.

Competitive training

Training for dancers who want to compete and perform. Dancebeat dancers take part in competitions, with recent successes celebrated on the studio’s Instagram.
