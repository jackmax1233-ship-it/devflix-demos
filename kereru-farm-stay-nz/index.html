Stay & train with your dog

A peaceful rural retreat for dogs who need to decompress

For some dogs the world is very chaotic and stressful — and getting away from it all at a quiet rural retreat is just what the doggy doctor ordered.

Solo came for a decompression stay at Kererū Cottage. Louis and Tato explored the trails, then recovered in front of the wood fire. Space, quiet, and room to be a dog.

Training support available through our friends at All Sorts Dog Training.
