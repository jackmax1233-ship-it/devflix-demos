Treatments

Medical-grade skin solutions.

Every treatment below is offered at the clinic — details are taken from our own treatment guides. For suitability and pricing, call or DM us.

Glow Peels

Chemical peels that exfoliate dead skin cells, target dullness and uneven tone, and reveal brighter, smoother skin.

Laser Hair Removal

Concentrated light energy targets hair follicles for long-term hair reduction — for face, underarms, arms, legs and full body.

Face PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

Your own platelet-rich plasma stimulates natural healing — used for acne scars and skin rejuvenation, with subcision where suitable.

Botox — Upper Face

Softens forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet for smoother expressions and a fresher, youthful look.

Botox — Lower Face

Targets the masseter and chin muscles for a slimmer, more defined jawline — define, slim, smooth, rejuvenate.

Microneedling / Derma Pen

Boosts collagen, reduces imperfections, pores and fine lines, and refines texture for a natural, radiant glow.

Face Exosomes

Advanced skin rejuvenation that stimulates cell repair, boosts collagen and elastin, and restores a youthful glow.

BB Glow Facial

Brightens dull skin, evens skin tone, minimizes pores and gives a healthy, natural radiance.
