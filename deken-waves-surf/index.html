Your coach

Simon Deken

Simon runs Deken Waves surf coaching out of Oakura on the Taranaki coast — an experienced local surfing coach who knows the breaks, the banks and the best small-wave days for learners.

Sessions are small, hands-on and built around fun: Simon moves through the group in the water, guiding and offering tips while caregivers surf alongside the youngest groms.
