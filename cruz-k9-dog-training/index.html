The method

Balanced training. Honest results. No gimmicks.

Cruz K9 specialises in behaviour modification — the dogs other trainers turn away: reactivity, aggression, dogs that can't be walked in public. The approach is balanced and direct, built on exposure, neutrality and clear handling.

Services

Three ways to train with us

Every route starts with a conversation — message on Instagram and we'll point you at the right one for your dog.

Foundation

1-to-1 Sessions

Private, focused work on your dog's specific issues — reactivity, aggression, recall, lead manners. You and the trainer, no distractions.

Exposure

Group Classes

Public group sessions built around raw exposure to triggers — no tricks, no fancy footwork. Owners develop handling skills with real dogs around them.

Socialisation

Pack Walks

Structured group walks with like-minded owners. Reactive dogs welcome — build confidence, neutrality and social skills out on the trail.

New: Drop & Train

Short on time or working through something serious? The trainer collects your dog — or you drop them off — and they train through the day, then hand the progress back to you.

Behaviour case

Bitten both owners. Eight weeks later: zero incidents.

Finn is a five-year-old cockapoo whose owners asked for help after he had bitten both of them in the home — resource guarding that had them walking on eggshells. Eight weeks after the first session: not a single incident, no more biting or lunging, the guarding stopped.

If your dog has been labelled "too aggressive", "unfixable" or "too much" — you're exactly who these sessions are for.

Caesar

Rottweiler · First session at 5 months

Caesar arrived as a five-month-old pup already barking and growling at dogs — and heading for 50–60kg as an adult. His owner wanted it stopped before it became a problem.

First session: foundation, engagement, neutrality and socialisation, working alongside veteran student Loki. By the end of it, Caesar was ignoring every other dog they met.

Source: @cruz_k9_dogtraining, 20 Oct 2026

Ragz

Rottweiler · Rescue behaviour case

Ragz was rescued from the meat trade in China. When his owners came to Cruz K9, his behaviour was the detailed subject of a full case study — the kind of dog most trainers won't take on.

Rescue dogs with serious histories are a speciality here, not an exception.
