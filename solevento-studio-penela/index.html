Slow down, get your hands in clay, in Penela.

Solevento Studio is an open ceramics studio in Penela, in central Portugal — a place for cosy, creative mornings at the wheel and the workbench, and for making something special for your home with your own hands.

Message Solevento on Instagram

An open studio, not a shop window

Solevento describes itself simply: ceramics & pottery workshops, an open studio — estúdio aberta — in Penela. The studio also makes handmade ceramics, shared under its sister account for ceramic pieces. Everything here is made by hand, in the studio, at a pace the studio itself calls slow and peaceful: “a cosy, creative morning” where you slow down and make something special and inspiring for your home.

Workshops & classes

Pottery workshops

Hands-on ceramic workshops at the Penela studio — from a first morning with clay to themed sessions such as bowl-making and decorative techniques. Message the studio to book your spot.

Regular art classes

Regular art classes run at the studio, including classes for children — a place, in the studio’s own words, “for curious hands and busy imaginations”.

Gift vouchers

The studio offers gift vouchers for special days — a creative gift for someone who would rather make something than receive something.

How a visit works

Workshops are announced on the studio’s Instagram, and places are booked by direct message. If you are planning a trip to Penela, write ahead — the studio will tell you what is running and when.

No prices are listed here: workshop details are confirmed with the studio when you book.

Visit & book

Solevento Studio is in Penela, Portugal. There is no online booking page — the way to reach the studio is Instagram, where new workshops and classes are announced first.
