From the van

More than just a coffee stop.

Alongside the coffee and cocktails, the van serves up sourdough toasties and icy frappes — the kind of thing that keeps a crowd at a game or a gathering happily fed.

Community

A van with a big heart.

Bella & Co isn't just a coffee stop — the team runs community fundraisers too. Their Walk for Bell rubber duck race raised funds for the Code 9 Foundation, honouring the memory of their beautiful Bell, with ducks adopted by family and friends for a fun day by the river.

It's the same spirit they bring to every event they set up at: local, friendly, and for the community.
